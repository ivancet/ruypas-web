Conditions & FAQ

Who can drive?

Drivers aged 25+ with at least 2 years of licence. ID/passport and valid licence required.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, shown on each car (transfer or cash), fully refunded when the car is returned in the same condition.

What if I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 48 h before pick-up. After that, 50% of the first day is charged.

Fuel and mileage?

Full-to-full policy. Each car includes daily km; extra km are charged at the listed rate.

Is it insured?

The car has mandatory insurance. I'll tell you the exact cover and excess before confirming.

Are prices final?

No: prices shown are indicative and confirmed when the booking is accepted.
